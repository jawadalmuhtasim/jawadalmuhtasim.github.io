About me

I'm Jawad and I'm a

I am Jawad Al Muhtasim, a student of English Literature at the University of Rajshahi, driven by a quiet resolve to grow, create, and contribute.

I believe learning isn’t confined to classrooms; it lives in conversations, challenges, and the choices we make each day. As Marcus Aurelius wrote, "Very little is needed to make a happy life; it is all within yourself." Whether it's exploring ideas, solving problems, teaching others, volunteering, or building alongside a team - I value work that sharpens the mind and strengthens the soul.

I’m drawn to stories, systems, and people especially where creativity meets clarity, and effort creates real value. I’m not in a hurry to impress, but I do strive to improve: with sincerity, discipline, and purpose. As T.S. Eliot once said, “Only those who will risk going too far can possibly find out how far one can go.”

Like any worthwhile story, this one is still being written. What you’ll find here is not a final draft, but scattered pages, notes from a journey shaped by effort, curiosity, and quiet persistence. I build with care, not speed; with meaning, not noise. This space holds the fragments of that unfolding narrative - not to impress, but to trace a path. One honest line at a time.
