About me

I'm Jawad and I'm a

I first built this website around 2019 or 2020 during Covid, when I was teaching myself to code in my spare time on someone else's laptop, working through YouTube tutorials and trying to make each new idea function. I thought of it as practice, not as something worth preserving; I certainly did not imagine that years later someone might arrive here and read it.

Back then, I was just a 14y old boy from Manikganj. Since then, I've moved to Dhaka to study at St. Joseph Higher Secondary School and eventually made my way to the University of Rajshahi (EDRU'25). As I update this page, I'm still a student at RU, though that may have changed by the time you read this.

I am still learning, usually by asking questions and discovering how much I have yet to understand. The Delphic injunction know thyself sounds simple until one tries to live by it. For me, it is the daily work of examining my motives, owning my mistakes, and beginning again. I think of eudaimonia not as a promise of constant fulfilment, but as an ideal worth pursuing. Achievement matters, though I do not want it to become the sole measure by which I judge a life. I would rather preserve my curiosity, treat others with kindness, and live a life without regrets.

I have kept this page because it preserves a trace of the fourteen-year-old who first built it, while allowing me to add some context from the perspective of the person I have since become. This page is simply a more personal introduction than a name or username alone can offer: a little context, a little history, and a glimpse of the person behind the screen. If you've found your way here, by chance or otherwise, welcome. If you'd like to know me a little better or get in touch, you'll find my social profiles and contact details below.
